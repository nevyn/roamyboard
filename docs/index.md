# Docs

- [Glossary](glossary.md): canonical terms for modules, the case, the joint and the firmware, with a labelled diagram.
- [Firmware](firmware.md): the ZMK module and config in `zmk/`: how the chain is read, key module count and anchors, the builds, the socket board pins, building and flashing, and updating the halves over Bluetooth with `roamy-ota` or a phone.
- [PDA manual](pda/index.html): the micro PDA for its users: opening it, the home screen, and every app with its keys and a screenshot. Published at https://roamyboard.nevyn.dev/pda/. The screenshots in `docs/pda/screens/` come from the screen mock: `zmk/tools/screen_mock/run.sh`, then `python3 zmk/tools/screen_mock/pda_screens.py` on its output.
- [Micro PDA](pda.md): the PDA on the right half's display: the PDA and Note keys, the note list and editor, how PDA mode keeps keys from the host, the split data channel's GATT service and messages, typing a note out, and the known gaps.
- [MIDI](midi.md): the BLE-MIDI service on the central, the MIDI keys and layers, the MIDI view on the right half's display, `roamy-midi` for checking it from a Mac, and the known gaps.
- [Landing page](index.html): what the Roamyboard is, a hero gallery (photos in `docs/photos`, cropped to 3:2 and stripped of their metadata, and the 3D view of a half; `hero.js` keeps the slide in `?slide=`), photos of the build so far, and links to the three pages below. `.github/workflows/pages.yml` publishes `docs/` as the site root whenever a page changes on main: https://roamyboard.nevyn.dev/ is this page, and each page below is at its folder name (`/assembly/`, `/order/`, `/layout/`). The pages' relative paths never leave `docs/`, so the same files work served from the repo root: `git lfs pull` (the 3D models are in Git LFS), run `python3 -m http.server` in the repo root and open `/docs/`. The pages fetch the keymaps from `docs/keymaps`, a symlink to `zmk/config`, so there is no copy of them; the workflow publishes copies at `/keymaps/`. The old `/docs/<page>/` URLs redirect to the new ones, keeping the query and the anchor.
- [Assembly guide](assembly/index.html): printing, soldering, assembling, flashing and testing a half, with 3D views from the Cadova model. Published at https://roamyboard.nevyn.dev/assembly/.
- [Order page](order/index.html): the configurator a buyer picks a build on — halves, columns, rows, curve, case colour, switches and keycaps — with a line-by-line price in euros and kronor and a `mailto:` that carries the configuration as a link. `docs/parts.js` holds the parts and their vendor prices, shared with the assembly guide's parts list; `docs/order/catalog.js` holds the filament, switch and keycap catalogues, the assembly rate, and `MARGIN`, the share added to every part's vendor price on the bill; `docs/order/geometry.js` ports the case model's arc so the curve shown is the curve the case prints at; `docs/order/keys.js` maps a module position to its keymap column, the same mapping the layout page draws, so the page can letter the keys and paint by key group. Published at https://roamyboard.nevyn.dev/order/. `node --test docs/order/order.test.js` checks the geometry against `case/Case design.md` and that no part reaches the bill unpriced.
- [Keyboard layout](layout/index.html): the default layout, layer by layer, drawn as two halves of modules with a key module count per half. The page fetches and parses `zmk/config/roamyboard_split.keymap` (through `docs/keymaps`) when it loads, so it has no copy of the layout. Published at https://roamyboard.nevyn.dev/layout/. `node --test docs/layout/keymap.test.js` checks the parser and that every binding in both keymaps has a label.
